How to read this

Three textbook models feed the table above: a GARCH(1,1) forecast of tomorrow's volatility (Vol 1d/ann), an Ornstein–Uhlenbeck fit of how far the price has stretched from its own fitted 1-year mean (OU z-score) and how many days that stretch takes to halve (Half-life), and a plain 5-day percent change (Mom 5d). The Verdict column applies these, in order: an earnings date within 3 calendar days flags avoid (event-risk, since the vol forecast likely understates an earnings move); a z-score of ≥1.5σ alongside ≥35% annualized vol flags avoid (mean-reversion-watch); under 20% vol with 5-day momentum beyond ±2% flags bullish or bearish (trend-watch); anything else is neutral. Every verdict traces back to these three numbers — nothing here is hidden or proprietary. These are research labels, not trade instructions. Research only — not investment advice.
